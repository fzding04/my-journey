Today's day 1

I discovered [the gender dysphoria bible](https://genderdysphoria.fyi/en) this morning, and it's tipped my opinions from "idk" to "probably trans".

It's really hard, and I talked to my (now) ex about it, because we're still close and we only broke up last week. I really wish that I could be the man that she wants and needs, but although I was him for 2 years, there were really hard parts. When we were together, by each other's side, I was *so* happy. I felt so good just from holding her hand, and kissing her lit me up from inside. But I ***was*** him for 2 years, so when I wasn't around her, I acted how he felt he should act. And when she wasn't around, it was so uncomfortable at times, to want to dress a certain way, and to be so large and take up so much space, but I also felt immense guilt at feeling these things, and I would try and suppress them.

I still get deep feelings of uncertainty, and I do right now (17:54). I feel so selfish, to prioritise my happiness over the expectations of everybody else, and I doubt whether this is worth it, or if I should have just kept as I was, because what I had with her was so good. We used to be so happy. 

But also, in retrospect, I used to want to talk to the girls even in early primary school, and spend time with them. I spent time with boys too, not to dismiss that, but I remember lingering behind after all the other boys had run off to PE one time because I wanted to talk to and go with the girls. Maybe this is nothing, but the most prominent narrative for many trans girls is the whole "always knew they were trans", so I suppose those are my two cents.

I want to document how I feel so that I can look back when I'm older or when I'm doubting myself, and I hope that my best friend can understand where I'm at when she reads this.
