I came out to my mum today.

Was it good? Not really. She hit me, guns blazing, with the classics, such as "you're selfish" and "you're hurting your parents" and "you'll never be pretty" and "it's just a phase", but she also dropped some fire, more novel arguments such as "you're just being weak" and "you'll be in [physical] pain [from procedures] for the rest of your life". It was pretty hurtful, and I had a cry on my own in the kitchen in the afternoon, but it wasn't so bad because Buns was holding my hand off-camera while I came out, and I wasn't kicked out, so it's not so bad. Honestly it was such a massive heartbreak, but also I don't think it could have gone another way. The conclusion is that I'm not gonna bring it up and I don't think she will either, for at least like half a year probably more.

I think that, in terms of my transition, this isn't ideal, and I doubt that they will ever encourage me, because now I know that my mum thinks that I'm just "torturing" myself and doing something terrible and irreparably wrong. But if I save over the next two years, maybe I could start private hrt around the start of 6th year, and by the time they realise I will be fully independent? I think that I could work really hard and start earlier, but realistically I think that it's probably not worth it.

Tactically, I think that I will save up so that I can, and keep seeing the GP just to establish this sorta history of engagement and commitment, hopefully getting a fertility referral. I'm not even super sure that I want to have my sperm frozen, because it's honestly more as a way to appease my parents than anything, especially since that I suspect I will end up with a man, and I don't really care about having biological children because I'm more than happy to adopt.

It's definitely a problem for the future, and I will be sad but work on stuff I can work on, like getting good grades for my career and figuring out my wardrobe and my style and my makeup and stuff.

I did manage to batch cook 15 burritos, so that's a w. It'll keep me going for at least a week and a half, in combination with random stuff I buy lol.
