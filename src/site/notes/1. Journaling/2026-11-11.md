I had my meeting with my tutor about being trans and stuff. Honestly? It was kinda useless. I think that he's kind of new to being a tutor, and being openly trans and asking for help with it is quite a new phenomenon?

I was hoping that the college would provide financial support if my parents just cut me off, but it seems that that might not be available as much as I'd hope it would be. I think they'd help with 2/3 thousand for maintenance, which would help contribute towards accommodation, but my tutor kind of just waved his hands and said that all the students that he's aware of have had happy endings. I'm sure he's well meaning, and there's an element to truth there, but I can't help but feel like that wasn't what I wanted from that meeting.

I haven't been able to decide on when I should come out to my parents. My options are now, when I get to Singapore (I'm visiting them for the holidays), just before I leave to return, further in the future.

I think that, for me, I will need to make sure to do a couple of things. First, I HAVE to pass all of my exams, because I can't repeat anything. This has always been a priority, but I feel like it's more of a priority than ever. Secondly, I need to start saving up and making preparations, if they do cut me off.
